# Kapyn — LinkedIn Outreach Drafts

Personalized cold outreach to people working in (or creating content about) AI & tech, asking them to try Kapyn and give feedback.

## What this channel is for
A **feedback + champion-finding** channel — good for your first 50–100 users and 5–10 people who'll genuinely share it. Not a growth engine (that's the SEO knowledge base). Realistic reply rate if personalized: **~5–15%**.

**Three rules or it backfires:**
1. **Target by leverage** — prioritize people with an audience (creators, newsletter writers, frequent AI posters). One share > one user.
2. **Personalize the first line for real** — reference one specific, true thing about their work. The rest can be templated. This single line is the difference between ~12% and ~1% replies.
3. **Warm up first** — leave a genuine comment on a couple of their posts before you DM. Cold → familiar roughly doubles replies.

**The LinkedIn way = two steps.** Don't pitch in the connection request. Get the connection with a warm note, *then* send the ask. Drop the link from the first touch; share it in the follow-up.

## Step 1 — Connection request notes
LinkedIn caps this field at **300 characters**. No link. Pick one and swap the `[bracket]`.

### Note A — humble / feedback-first
```
Hi [Name] — your AI breakdowns are some of the clearest I've come across.
I've been building something small in the same space (keeping up with AI
without the noise) and would love your read on it. Mind if I connect?
```

### Note B — builder-to-builder
```
Hi [Name] — fellow builder in the AI space. I made a tiny app for keeping
up with AI without drowning in it, and I trust your taste on this kind of
thing. Would love to connect and hear what you think.
```

### Note C — short & direct
```
Hi [Name] — really like your work on [topic]. Built a small AI-news app
I think you'd have a sharp opinion on. Mind connecting?
```

### Note D — problem-led
```
Hi [Name] — you clearly live in the AI firehose like I do. I built a small
thing to make keeping up less exhausting and would value your honest read.
Open to connecting?
```

```

model releases, launches, research. It turns the day's big developments
into 30-second swipeable reads, each with a short "why it matters."

If you've got two minutes, I'd genuinely love your honest feedback —
what's the first thing you'd change?

kapyn.app
```

### Message B — peer / "steal it if useful"
```
Thanks for connecting, [Name].

Quick one — I got tired of juggling five newsletters and a dozen tabs to
keep up with AI, so I built Kapyn: the day's major AI news as 30-second
swipeable reads, each with a "why it matters."

Sharing in case it's useful to you, and because I'd value your eye on it.
Anything that feels off or missing?

kapyn.app
```

### Post A — origin story

## Personalization checklist (20 seconds per person)
- [ ] Read their last 2–3 posts / their headline / their company
- [ ] Pick ONE specific, true thing to reference in line 1
- [ ] (Optional) leave a genuine comment on a recent post before DMing
- [ ] Send connection note (Step 1) — no link
- [ ] After accept, send follow-up (Step 2) — one link only

## Simple tracking
Keep a sheet: Name · Profile · Type (creator/builder/founder) · Warmed up? · Sent date · Replied? · Tried it? · Feedback. Review weekly — kill what's not landing, double down on what is.

## Don'ts
- Don't mass-blast identical messages (LinkedIn rate-limits, and people can tell).
- Don't put two links / heavy formatting in the first touch.
- Don't follow up more than once if they don't reply.
- Don't lead with "please use my app" — lead with their work + a feedback ask.
